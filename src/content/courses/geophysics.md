## Course overview

This introductory course examines the physical structure and evolution of the solid Earth through gravity, heat, geoelectricity, and geomagnetism. Plate tectonics provides the framework for relating these fields to the present Earth and its geological history. The emphasis is on understanding the Earth's interior rather than exploration practice. Seismology is covered in a separate course.

## Audience and preparation

The course is intended primarily for second-year students in Earth and Environmental Sciences who are new to geophysics. A background in Earth science and introductory calculus is required. Mathematical derivations are explained step by step during lectures.

## Topic sequence

1. **Earth and plate tectonics** - The physical Earth and the plate-tectonic framework.
2. **Gravity** - Fundamentals of the Earth's gravity field.
3. **Heat** - The Earth's thermal field and geothermal structure.
4. **Geoelectricity and geomagnetism** - Fundamentals of electrical and magnetic fields in the Earth.

## Materials and references

Lecture slides are the main course material, supported by the following textbooks:

- *Fundamentals of Geophysics*, 2nd and 3rd editions. Cambridge University Press.
- C. M. R. Fowler, *The Solid Earth: An Introduction to Global Geophysics*, 2nd edition. Cambridge University Press.
