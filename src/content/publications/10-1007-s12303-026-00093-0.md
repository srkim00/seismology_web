summaryEn: "Teleseismic P-wave directivity and stacking were analyzed for Mw 5.7–7.3 earthquakes at depths of 300–600 km in the northwestern Japan subduction zone. Smaller events generally rupture faster, whereas larger multi-subevent events rupture more slowly; the corrected duration–moment slope is about 0.26 and inferred rupture lengths span 8.6–30.9 km."
