summaryEn: "Pseudodynamic source ensembles reproducing one- and two-point statistics of dynamic rupture models were coupled to 3-D wave propagation for the southern Korean Peninsula. For the 2016 Mw 5.4 Gyeongju earthquake, the simulations reproduced surface waves, rupture directivity, and local and regional amplification, with mean and standard deviation of logarithmic PGV residuals of −0.28 and 0.78; Mw 6.0, 6.5, and 7.0 scenarios were also evaluated."
