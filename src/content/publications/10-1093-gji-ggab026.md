summaryEn: "Three years of ambient noise from 20 temporary and three permanent Jeju stations provided 2–15 s Rayleigh- and Love-wave group and phase velocities for transdimensional hierarchical Bayesian joint inversion of 1–10 km isotropic and radial-anisotropic structure. Negative anisotropy above 2 km and below 5 km is interpreted as dykes and vertical conduits, whereas up to 5% positive anisotropy at 2–5 km is interpreted as a horizontal sill complex."

researchQuestion: "Resolve the vertical and horizontal organization of Jeju upper-crustal magma plumbing from shear velocity and radial anisotropy."
dataAndRegion: "Rayleigh- and Love-wave group and phase velocities at 2–15 s from about three years of ambient noise at 20 temporary and three permanent stations were used."
methods: "Surface-wave tomography and transdimensional hierarchical Bayesian joint inversion of 1–10 km isotropic and radial-anisotropic structure were performed."
