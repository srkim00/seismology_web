summaryEn: "Surface-wave dispersion from MASW and microtremor arrays was jointly inverted at 21 permanent accelerometer stations in southeastern Korea to obtain probabilistic shear-wave velocity profiles to depths of as much as 1 km. The profiles generally agree with borehole measurements and resonance estimates from 1-D transfer functions and HVSR, and imply NEHRP site classes B–D."

researchQuestion: "Estimate deep shear-wave velocity profiles and probabilistic site classes for accelerometer stations in southeastern Korea."
