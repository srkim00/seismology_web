MODEL FORMULATION

Equations and interpretation limits

Potential temperature

dT/dz = αgT/Cp
T(z) = (Tp + 273.15) exp(αgz/Cp) − 273.15
Tp = (T(z) + 273.15) exp(−αgz/Cp) − 273.15

Tp remains constant only for reversible, adiabatic motion without melting, mixing, conduction, or dissipative heating.

Conductive lithosphere

T = Ts + (q0/kc)z − (Ac/2kc)z²

The model solves q0 so that the conductive profile meets the scenario temperature at the LAB, then uses the mantle adiabat below it. The NE Japan preset adds a Gaussian cooling anomaly as a schematic one-dimensional proxy.

Melting potential

Tsol = 1085.7 + 132.9P − 5.1P²
F = [(T − Tsol)/(Tlherz − Tsol)]1.5

P is in GPa and temperature is in °C. The hydrous control applies an explicit solidus depression. Full hydrous melting requires coupled water partitioning and an iterative solution.

Density and velocity response

Δρ/ρ ≈ −αΔT + Δρcomposition/ρ
ΔV/V = CTΔT + Cφφ + Ccomposition

The coefficients are user-visible assumptions. Pressure, mineralogy, water, melt geometry, anelasticity, grain size, frequency, and anisotropy make velocity-to-temperature conversion non-unique.
