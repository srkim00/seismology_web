Optional details: model and equation

V = ρI / (2π) × (1/rA − 1/rB)
ΔV = VM − VN = ρI / (2πa)
ρa = 2πa ΔV / I

Electrodes lie at x = −1.5a, −0.5a, +0.5a, +1.5a. The potential is the sum of a surface point source and a surface point sink in a homogeneous half-space. Distances rA and rB are in metres; I is in amperes. The return electrode is B.

The point-electrode model is singular exactly at A and B. The map masks points within 0.08a of either source, and saturates its displayed color above four times |VM|. The surface profile is shown only between M and N, away from those singularities. Measurement calculations are not clipped.

In heterogeneous ground the same calculation gives apparent resistivity. It need not equal the resistivity of any one layer.

Theory: Lowrie, Fundamentals of Geophysics, Chapter 4, §4.3 (pp. 252–276); UBC GeoSci: DC resistivity principles.
